- **Problem Justification:** Subscription churn causes a **$4.2M recurring ARR attrition**, with high acquisition costs ($380 CAC) vs retention ($65 CRC).
- **Architecture & Methodology:** 6-stage industry-standard CRISP-DM lifecycle (Business Understanding, Data Ingestion, EDA, Feature Engineering, Multi-Class Ensemble Modeling, and Closed-Loop Deployment).
- **Time Allocation:** Precisely budgeted across **32.5 focused hours** spanning 6 operational phases.
- **Python Technology Stack:** 
  - *Data Ingestion & Manipulation:* Pandas, NumPy, SQLAlchemy, PyArrow
  - *EDA & Statistical Modeling:* SciPy, Statsmodels, Matplotlib, Seaborn
  - *Machine Learning & Ensembles:* Scikit-Learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, Imbalanced-learn
  - *Explainability & Validation:* SHAP, LIME, Yellowbrick
  - *Deliverable Generation:* python-docx, Pillow
